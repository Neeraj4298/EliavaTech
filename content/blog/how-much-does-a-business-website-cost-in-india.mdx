## Why website quotes vary so widely

A website is not a single standardized product. The engineering effort depends on three main questions:

1. **What job must the website do?** A 5-page credibility site has very different requirements from a 500-product e-commerce store with Razorpay and GST invoicing.
2. **How fast and search-ready must it be?** Page speed, mobile responsiveness, and structured SEO data determine whether local customers find you on Google.
3. **Who maintains it after launch?** Domains, SSL certificates, backups, and content updates require ongoing care.

## Third-party and ongoing costs to budget for

Any honest technology partner will separate one-time build fees from third-party infrastructure costs that you pay directly to vendors:

- **Domain name (.com or .in):** Typically ₹800 to ₹1,200 per year via Cloudflare or your registrar.
- **Hosting & database:** Modern cloud tiers often start free or low-cost and scale with traffic.
- **Payment gateway fees:** Standard per-transaction fees charged by Razorpay (typically ~2% per transaction).
- **Monthly care plans:** If you prefer our team to handle backups, uptime monitoring, security updates, and content edits, our plans start from **₹1,499/month** (Essential), **₹2,999/month** (Growth), and **₹5,999/month** (Pro).
