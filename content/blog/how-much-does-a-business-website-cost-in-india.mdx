When business owners ask how much a website costs in India, quotes often range from ₹5,000 with a solo freelancer to several lakhs with a large agency. Knowing what separates each tier helps you pay for what your business needs today, without overpaying or getting stuck with a slow template.

## Why website quotes vary so widely

A website is not a single standard product. The effort depends on three main questions:

1. **What job must the website do?** A 5-page credibility site has very different requirements from a 500-product e-commerce store with Razorpay and GST invoicing.
2. **How fast must it be, and how easily should people find it?** Page speed, mobile layout and SEO setup decide whether local customers find you on Google.
3. **Who maintains it after launch?** Domains, SSL certificates, backups and content updates need ongoing care.

## Third-party and ongoing costs to budget for

An honest technology partner keeps one-time build fees separate from third-party costs that you pay directly to vendors:

- **Domain name (.com or .in):** Typically ₹800 to ₹1,200 per year via Cloudflare or your registrar.
- **Hosting and database:** Modern cloud tiers often start free or low-cost and scale with traffic.
- **Payment gateway fees:** Standard per-transaction fees charged by Razorpay (typically around 2% per transaction).
- **Monthly care plans:** If you prefer our team to handle backups, uptime monitoring, security updates, and content edits, our plans start from **₹1,499/month** (Essential), **₹2,999/month** (Growth), and **₹5,999/month** (Pro).
