Most growing businesses in India start managing enquiries the same way: a combination of Excel spreadsheets, notebooks, and individual salesperson WhatsApp chats. For your first 20 leads a month, that works fine.

When enquiry volume grows to 100+ leads a month across property listings, B2B catalogues, or service campaigns, spreadsheets quietly start leaking revenue.

## Four signs you have outgrown spreadsheets

You do not need a complex enterprise software suite on day one, but you do need a structured CRM when you notice these four symptoms:

1. **Follow-ups depend on memory:** Salespeople forget to call back a prospect who asked for a quote three days ago.
2. **Leads live on personal phones:** When a team member is on leave or leaves the company, customer conversation history disappears with them.
3. **No visibility into conversion stages:** You cannot see at a glance how many enquiries are in *New*, *Site Visit Scheduled*, *Negotiation*, or *Won*.
4. **Duplicate data entry:** Staff copy and paste names and phone numbers from website emails into WhatsApp and then into an invoice sheet.

## Off-the-shelf SaaS CRM vs. a focused custom CRM

Off-the-shelf international CRMs charge per user every month and often include hundreds of menus your staff will never touch. Because the interface feels overwhelming, teams stop updating it within weeks.
