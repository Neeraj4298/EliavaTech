Most growing businesses in India start managing enquiries the same way: a mix of Excel sheets, notebooks and each salesperson's own WhatsApp chats. For your first 20 leads a month, that works fine.

When enquiries grow to more than 100 leads a month, from property listings, B2B catalogues or service campaigns, spreadsheets quietly start costing you revenue.

## Four signs you have outgrown spreadsheets

You do not need complex enterprise software on day one. But you do need a proper CRM when you notice these four signs:

1. **Follow-ups depend on memory:** Salespeople forget to call back a customer who asked for a quote three days ago.
2. **Leads live on personal phones:** When a team member is on leave or leaves the company, customer conversation history disappears with them.
3. **No visibility into conversion stages:** You cannot see at a glance how many enquiries are in *New*, *Site Visit Scheduled*, *Negotiation*, or *Won*.
4. **Duplicate data entry:** Staff copy names and phone numbers from website emails into WhatsApp, and then into an invoice sheet.

## Ready-made CRM or a simple custom CRM

Ready-made international CRMs charge per user every month. They often have hundreds of menus your staff will never use. The screens feel overwhelming, so teams stop updating them within weeks.

### Separate views for owners and staff
