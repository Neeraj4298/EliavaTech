When reception staff are busy attending walk-in patients, missed calls turn into lost appointments—and manual reminder calls rarely happen on time.

## Where manual appointment booking breaks down

Appointment-based businesses typically face three operational bottlenecks:

- **After-hours enquiries go unanswered:** Patients often look for a doctor or dentist after 8:00 PM when the reception desk is closed.
- **High no-show rates:** Without an automated reminder 2 hours before the visit, patients forget or arrive late.
- **Fragmented patient records:** Enquiries arrive across phone calls, personal WhatsApp numbers, and website forms with no central log.

## What an automated WhatsApp booking flow looks like

Instead of forcing patients to download an app or fill out a long web form, WhatsApp automation meets them in the app they already use every day.

### Step 1: Instant greeting and department selection

When a patient sends *"Hi"* to your clinic's official WhatsApp number, the system replies within two seconds with a clean menu: choose a doctor or treatment, check timings, or request the Google Maps clinic pin.

### Step 2: Real-time slot selection

The workflow checks available morning and evening slots and presents two or three clear options. Once the patient taps a slot and shares their name, the booking is locked in automatically.

### Step 3: Automated CRM sync and 2-hour reminders

Using workflow automation (such as n8n connected to your clinic dashboard or Google Calendar), the appointment is logged immediately. Two hours before the visit, the patient receives an automated reminder with the clinic address and a one-tap reschedule option.
